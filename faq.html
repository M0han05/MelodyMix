Frequently Asked Questions

How do I listen to music? +

Simply browse through the "Home" or "Explore" pages, and click any song card to start playback. The player bar at the bottom will display your current track.

What genres are available? +

MelodyMix offers a curated list across 4 main genres: Phonk, Motivation, Feel Good, and Melancholic, spanning 5 languages.

Is the platform mobile-friendly? +

Absolutely. Our platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktops alike.

Can I search for specific artists? +

Yes, the search bar at the top of the Home page allows you to search for both song titles and artist names dynamically.
